# Scenario source register

**Accessed 2026-09-16.** Research for maritime scenario library v1.0.0. URLs identify evidence, not permission to reproduce photographs, maps or articles. Only original scenario prose and short factual paraphrases are included. Source material is not an expert-policy dataset. Official statements are strong evidence of what their issuer said, not automatically an impartial incident reconstruction.

## SC01

[USNI News: Philippine Sailor Severely Injured, Vessels Damaged as Chinese Block South China Sea Mission](https://news.usni.org/2024/06/17/philippine-sailor-severely-injured-vessels-damaged-as-chinese-block-south-china-sea-mission), Aaron-Matthew Lariosa, **2024-06-17**. Read article body, especially opening injury/damage paragraphs and the Chinese/Philippine account comparison. Independent reporting quoting officials; supplies the June 17 anchor, uncertainty over vessel involvement, and competing accounts. Lead photo describes an earlier encounter. Exact tracks, complete orders and a neutral casualty ledger were not established.

## SC02

[Philippine DFA: Foreign Ministers Support PH, China's De-Escalation Efforts in the South China Sea](https://dfa.gov.ph/authentication-functions/78-newsroom/dfa-releases/35203-foreign-ministers-support-ph-china-s-de-escalation-efforts-in-the-south-china-sea), **2024-07-28**. Used the dated opening and paragraphs describing the provisional rotation/resupply agreement and commitments. Full substantive text was exposed in the search result; direct opening failed. The [Philippine mission's July 21 statement listing](https://www.genevapm.dfa.gov.ph/national-statements/1113-philippins-and-china-agree-on-arrangement-for-rotation-and-resupplying-rore-to-brp-sierra-madre-on-ayungin-shoal) corroborates publication of an agreement announcement, but its image-only statement was not transcribed. Do not infer undisclosed terms.

## SC03

[PRC Foreign Ministry: Remarks on Reaching Provisional Arrangement with the Philippines on Managing the Situation at Ren'ai Jiao](https://www.mfa.gov.cn/mfa_eng/xw/fyrbt/fyrbt/202407/t20240730_11463281.html), page states **2024-07-22** despite a later date in the URL. Read the single Q&A, particularly the final paragraph on humanitarian resupply and the preceding stated conditions. Evidence of China's position and announcement; not proof that Manila accepted each stated condition. No current continuation of the agreement was verified.

## SC04

[Japan MLIT: Minister Maehara press conference](https://www.mlit.go.jp/report/interview/daijin100914.html), **2010-09-14**, Japanese. Read opening statement headed 閣議・閣僚懇, the paragraphs naming the trawler and Mizuki. Establishes Japan's account of the September 7 collision, September 8 arrest and September 9 referral. English scenario text is an original short paraphrase, not an official translation. Responsibility and sovereignty assertions remain attributed to Japan.

## SC05

[Nippon.com: The Senkaku Crisis in Perspective—Interview with Former Chief Cabinet Secretary Sengoku Yoshito](https://www.nippon.com/en/currents/d00365/), English publication **2017-12-05**, Japanese original **2017-11-15**. Read interview and September 2010 chronology. Retrospective participant account supports the political dimension and September 24 release. Its chronology groups the arrest with September 7; SC04 explicitly dates arrest to September 8 and is used for that detail. Memory and editorial selection are limitations. No original police evidence set, complete orders or independently reconstructed tracks were retrieved.

## SC06

[Japan MOFA: Search and Rescue regarding the collision of a Chinese fishing vessel and a cargo ship sailing under the flag of Greece](https://www.mofa.go.jp/press/release/press4e_001239.html), **2016-08-11**, as of 11:00 a.m., numbered paragraphs 1–2. Documents Japan's report of rescue of six fishermen around 67 km northwest of Uotsuri and diplomatic notification. This is a separate 2016 incident used only as precedent for the fictional rescue scenario; the game's four groups and focus-map location are invented.

## SC07

[Stena Bulk statements archive](https://www.stenabulk.com/statements-archive), **2019-07-19 to 2019-09-28**. Read July 19 statements 1–2 and the release/update chronology. Owner/manager source for vessel identity, initial loss of contact, 23 seafarers, and concern for crew welfare. Initial description of international waters differs from the later UK description of Omani waters. No statement is treated as ground-truth coordinates or a training demonstration.

## SC08

[UK Foreign Secretary: Situation in the Gulf, statement to Parliament](https://www.gov.uk/government/speeches/situation-in-the-gulf-foreign-secretary-statement), delivered **2019-07-22**, translation update **2019-07-24**. Read opening incident narrative through passage-location paragraphs and later escort-notification passages. Primary source for the UK's account of seizure and Montrose's inability to reach the scene in time. Legal characterizations belong to the speaker. Support-arrival round, decision graph and game custody mechanic are not taken from this source.

## SC09

[US MARAD: Advisory 2023-005, Various—GPS Interference & AIS Spoofing](https://www.maritime.dot.gov/msci/2023-005-various-gps-interference-ais-spoofing), **2023**, §§2–3, cancellation §5. The retrieved page marks it **Cancelled**, with automatic expiry **2023-09-04**; no exact issue date was established from the page. Used only as historical evidence of interference reports in Hormuz and information-integrity concerns. No current threat level, source attribution or navigational recommendation is imported into game rules.

## SC10

[UN Security Council press statement on Yemen, SC/12541](https://press.un.org/en/2016/sc12541.doc.htm), **2016-10-04**, opening three paragraphs. Condemns a Houthi attack on a UAE vessel near Bab al-Mandeb on October 1 and calls for de-escalation. Full statement was available in the search response; a direct open returned an internal error. It does not itself name Swift or establish exact attack location, cargo or casualties. The page labels the press release as not an official record.

## SC11

[Reuters via gCaptain: Yemeni Rebels Destroy UAE Military Logistics Ship in Red Sea](https://gcaptain.com/yemen-rebels-destroy-uae-military-logistics-ship-in-red-sea/), **2016-10-03**, article paragraphs describing the vessel, coalition rescue account and Houthi account. Read substantive body. Establishes reporting of Swift, coalition claims of its humanitarian task/rescue, and the opposing military-vessel description. The headline/early destruction claim must not be converted into a verified sinking. No exact group or passenger count is used in the exercise.

## SC12

[WorkBoat: Former U.S. Navy HSV-2 Swift wrecked in Yemen missile attack](https://www.workboat.com/bluewater/hsv-2-swift-wrecked-yemen-missile-attack), Kirk Moore, **2016-10-07**, opening damage/afloat/salvage paragraphs, inspected in the search response. Supports the later damage-but-afloat distinction. No missile model, weapon performance or attack geometry is imported; those particulars are unnecessary for the post-incident exercise.
